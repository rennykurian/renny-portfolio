01About

I'm a Quality Assurance leader with experience spanning manual, automation, performance and AI/LLM testing. I've led test teams, designed automation frameworks and driven process improvement across banking, financial services, manufacturing and enterprise applications — in India and the United States.

Today I'm a Test Lead at S&P Global, where much of my work is validating AI features: designing evals, building Playwright automation for model outputs, and using Langfuse and LangSmith to manage datasets and observe AI pipelines.

Underneath it all is a habit from years of process consulting and audits: strong requirement analysis, clear reporting, and a bias for closing gaps before they reach production.

02Testing AI & LLMs

LLM features don't fail like traditional software — they drift, hallucinate and vary run to run. Here's how I approach quality for them.

  1. Prompt validation

    Functional and regression testing of prompts and LLM-based features, so changes to prompts or models don't quietly break behaviour.

  2. Automated evals

    Designing and running evaluation suites with automation scripts to check output quality and validate system scalability.

  3. Playwright for LLMs

    End-to-end Playwright automation that drives AI features through the UI and asserts on model outputs reliably.

  4. Observability

    Langfuse and LangSmith for dataset management, tracing and observability testing across AI pipelines.
